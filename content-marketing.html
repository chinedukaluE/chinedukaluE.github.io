How I work

The method

Five steps. I've led teams through all of them and done each one myself.

01

Listen

Find out who's buying, what they search for and what they still don't get about the product.

02

Position

Fix the core message first, so every article, email and post tells the same story.

03

Plan

Build pillars and a monthly calendar tied to search demand and the sales funnel.

04

Write and ship

Write it, edit it, optimise it for Google and AI answers, and publish on schedule.

05

Distribute and measure

Push it through social, email and outreach, then keep what works and cut what doesn't.
