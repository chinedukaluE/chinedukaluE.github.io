Front page · Content marketing

Five years of words that moved the numbers

A content writer and content marketer who plans the strategy, writes the pieces, fixes the site they live on and builds the emails that follow up.

By Chinedu Kalu Emmanuel · Senior Content, SEO & Growth Marketing

Most content teams don't fail because they can't write. They fail because the writing, the search work, the website and the follow-up live with four different people, and nobody joins them up. I'm usually the one brought in to join them up.

Since 2021 I've written and edited for data compliance firms, HR tech, fintech, ride-hailing, social commerce, iGaming, e-commerce, music and nonprofits.

The work has added up to more than a thousand published pieces: long-form SEO articles, web copy, email sequences, social posts and video scripts. Each one had a job to do, whether that was ranking, converting or keeping a customer around.

I also build. I write WordPress themes in PHP and HTML, rebuild CMSs in Framer, set up email automations in GoHighLevel and HubSpot, and design AI agents that take repetitive content work off a team's plate.

Editor's letter

Why hire me

On being the missing piece, not the perfect one.

"I don't need to be the loudest person on your team. I need to be the one who gets the content out and makes it count."

I'm not going to pretend I've done everything perfectly. What I've done is a lot of the unglamorous work that makes content pay off: rewriting a confusing product story until buyers get it, building the glossary page nobody else had time for, chasing the backlinks, setting up the email that goes out on day three.

Most of my career has been with startups, where there's rarely someone to hand things to. So I learned the whole chain, from strategy and calendars to writing, editing, SEO, publishing and reporting. I can lead a writing team, and I'm just as happy writing the article myself.

If your team has strong people but gaps between them, that's where I'm most useful.
